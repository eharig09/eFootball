# NFL film notes

[[06 Sports Betting/Football/NFL/NFL Dashboard|Game notebook]]

1. Run **NFL Game Notes Template** from the notebook. Choose **Select team → season → game**. Type a team name, enter the season, then choose a game by date, opponent, and score/status. Completed and upcoming games are included, and historical seasons can be entered. Selecting existing notes opens them without reimporting; the URL/ID option refreshes site data. Dates, opponents, and links fill automatically. You can also use an already imported game or create a manual game.
2. Open the game's note in **Live Preview / editing mode**. Write ordinary bullets under the team and Offense, Defense, or Context heading. No special syntax, forms, or tags are needed. `+` and `−` are optional text within your bullets.
3. Record shared context once: what portion of the tape you watched, personnel, weather, opponent strength, and score/game state where relevant. Add play timestamps, down/distance, or sources inside an individual bullet only when useful. `tape_url` is optional.
4. Open a team page to see its notes from every game in the selected season. Each entry retains its opponent, date, game link, shared context, and full text. Empty sections are skipped. Edit the original game note; team pages update automatically. Leave a team's season Property blank for all seasons.
5. Use **Open this game on the site**, the team's site link, or expand the folded **Site comparison**. Write your interpretation once under **Check against the site**: what agrees, what differs, and what to check next. Cached site data shows its import date; importing the same URL/ID refreshes it without replacing your tape notes. Past-game site data can include information known after the game.

The Games folder is your writing workspace. Matchups holds imported comparison data and earlier research. Existing observations retain their original matchup associations; they are available in a single folded section on team pages. They are not automatically labeled as tape observations or assigned to a different historical game.

The importer reuses the preferred or first supplied quote for comparison, preserving its source label. It does not ask you to enter a bet. The older betting, snapshot, and blog tools remain available through Earlier research; none of those fields are required for game notes.

Example bullet (hypothetical): “Q2 08:14, 3rd-and-6: late pressure forced a checkdown. Similar result on two earlier third downs; check whether the replacement guard is the common factor.” Write an initial interpretation and uncertainty in the same bullet rather than creating another record for it.
