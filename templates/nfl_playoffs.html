How to read this

What the model assumes

Games. Each remaining game uses the market line when one is posted, otherwise team ratings built from Elo, results and market lines. Totals use the posted total or the league average, so simulated games produce real scores. Overtime ties are rare but possible.

Tiebreakers. Division and wild-card ties follow the NFL procedures: head-to-head, division and conference records, common games, strength of victory and schedule, points rankings, then net points. Net touchdowns is not modelled; a coin toss settles anything still level. The same code reproduces the real 2010–2025 playoff fields, byes and home teams exactly.

Seeding and bracket. Division winners take seeds 1–4 by record, the next three by record are wild cards, the top seed gets the bye, and the bracket re-seeds each round with the Super Bowl at a neutral site.

Accuracy. Tested on 2015–2025 from weeks 4, 8 and 12, playoff probabilities are well calibrated: teams given 30–50% made it about 43% of the time and teams given 90%+ made it about 96% of the time. Early-season odds are necessarily wide.
