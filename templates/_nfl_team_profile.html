Schedule-adjusted profile

Method

Each offense and defense is rated separately on every measure from the games played so far, adjusted for the opposition it faced (the Margin Power construction, applied to the pieces a margin is made of). "Adjusted" is what the unit would do against an average opponent; "Schedule" is adjusted minus raw, so a positive number on offense means the schedule held the raw number down. Ranks run best first on quality measures; on pass rate and seconds per play, 1 is the most pass-heavy and the fastest.

Where the points come from

Method

Points per game are possessions × plays per possession × points per play. Each factor is the schedule-adjusted rating, set against the league average; the gap to the league is shared across the three in proportion to their log gaps, so the contributions add up to the whole gap. Special teams and turnovers are inside points per play, not separated out. On defense a negative contribution means fewer points allowed than the league.
