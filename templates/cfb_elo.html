How to read this

One rating, kept in context

Current Elo is CFBD's pregame Elo from the team's earliest upcoming rated game. If none remains, the latest available pregame observation is used. This avoids jumping ahead to a later scheduled game.

Weekly movement compares the current observation with the prior stored rating. Season movement compares it with the first stored rating of the season.

Season range is the lowest and highest pregame rating stored for the team. Conference strength is the simple average of current ratings for rated FBS members.
