Glossary — what do these terms mean?
Margin Power

Our primary point-margin projection, comparing the two teams' scoring and efficiency profiles. Shown as a z-score — how unusual the edge is compared to a typical week.

Structural

A confirmation signal requiring at least 2 of 3 team-strength inputs — our Football Lab rating, team Elo, and efficiency power — to agree with Margin Power's side.

Line Elo

A separate Elo system trained to fit betting-market behavior rather than game outcomes. Used as a check on whether the market itself is confirming the same side.

HC / QB Elo

A person-level Elo system that tracks head coaches and starting quarterbacks individually, carried across team changes. Gives a fourth, personnel-based read on the matchup.

True Elo

The raw team Elo rating from our play-by-play data provider (CFBD). Unrelated to Line Elo or our own coach/QB Elo systems — shown for reference.

Agreement (e.g. “3/4”)

How many of the four signals — Margin Power, Structural, Line Elo, HC/QB — point the same direction. Frozen routes require specific agreement patterns before producing a pick.

Route

A specific, frozen combination of agreement count and spread range that historically produced a real edge in 2020–2025 backtesting. Routes are locked and never changed after being discovered, to avoid fitting to results already seen.

Follow vs. Fade

Some routes back the side the signals point to (“follow”); others have historically performed better betting the opposite side (“fade”). Both are frozen, backtested behaviors — not live judgment calls.

Engine A vs. Engine B

Engine A is the quantitative convergence engine (the four signals above). Engine B is a separate narrative/overreaction engine looking for markets over- or under-reacting to a team's last result or a QB change. They can agree, disagree, or fire independently.

Totals (research only)

A signal we're tracking and measuring but haven't frozen into an action rule yet. Shown for context and future evaluation, not as a pick.
