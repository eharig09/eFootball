Method

From play-by-play. "Over expected" compares each early-down play with what the league does in the same spot (down, distance, field zone, win-probability state), so a team that trails a lot is not mistaken for a pass-happy one. Ranks run highest value first, among all teams that season. These describe choices; they are not a forecast, and they belong to the team's play-calling, which is the head coach's unless the staff table says another coordinator calls the plays. A coach's results against his opponent-adjusted ratings did not predict his next games, so nothing here moves a number.
