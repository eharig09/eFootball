Method

Personnel groups are running backs then tight ends (11 = 1 RB and 1 TE, 12 = 1 RB and 2 TE, 21 = 2 RB and 1 TE). Defensive packages count defensive backs: Base 4 or fewer, Nickel 5, Dime 6 or more. Field zones: backed up = inside the offense’s own 20; neutral = own 21 to own 49; plus = midfield to the opponent’s 21; red zone = the 20 to the 11; goal to go = the first-down line is the goal line. Field side is the wide side of the field, boundary is the sideline nearest the ball (the side of the hash). Routes are the targeted receiver’s route.
