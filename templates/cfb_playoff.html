How to read this

What the model assumes

Games. Each remaining game uses the market line when one is posted, otherwise team ratings built from Elo, results and market lines. Teams also get a season-long strength error, so one team being better (or worse) than rated carries across all its games.

Standings. Conference records, then head-to-head among tied teams, then committee strength. The top two teams play for the title in each conference ({{ forecast.params.title_game_conferences | length }} hold a championship game); the Sun Belt sends its two division winners. The Pac-12, American, Conference USA and Mountain West games are played on the higher seed's campus.

Committee. There is no published formula, so ranking is learned from the final committee rankings of 2023–25 using rating, strength of record, losses and conference titles. On seasons it was not trained on, it picked 10–11 of the 12 playoff teams each year.

Field and bracket. Under the 2026-27 rules the four Power 4 champions and the top-ranked Group of 6 champion get automatic bids, as does Notre Dame if it is ranked in the top 12. The rest are at-large. The top four ranked teams get byes, and an automatic qualifier ranked outside the top 12 is the bottom seed.
